Layout fit

Fit a split column-staggered layout to your hands at 1:1 scale. Import and export layout JSON files.

1. Calibrate

Hold a bank card (ID-1, 85.6 x 54 mm) against the screen, top-left corners aligned. Adjust until the orange line is hidden behind the card and the blue line is fully visible, touching the card edge. Judge on straight edges, real cards have rounded corners.

card goes here

2. Fit

  • drag a finger key: moves its whole column along the column direction
  • drag a thumb key: moves it. Click it, then slider or mouse wheel: rotates it
  • tilt rotates a whole half around its origin key (orange dot)
  • right half is stored in left-hand frame and drawn mirrored

3. Import / export

Text box shows current layout. Paste JSON into it then "Import from text box" to load. Accepts this tool's format plus older fit-result and fit-compare exports.
